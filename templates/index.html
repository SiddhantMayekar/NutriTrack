Empowering Healthier Communities

NutriTrack is a web-based Nutrition & Health Tracking platform designed to help individuals and communities build healthier lifestyles.

Monitor your meals, calculate BMI, track water intake, record physical activities, and receive valuable health insights— all from one simple and user-friendly platform.

Healthy Living Tips

Small healthy habits every day can lead to a healthier and happier life.

Healthy Diet

Eat balanced meals with fruits, vegetables, whole grains and protein every day.

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily to keep your body hydrated.

Stay Active

Walk, jog or exercise for at least 30 minutes every day.

Sleep Well

Get 7–8 hours of quality sleep to improve your physical and mental health.
